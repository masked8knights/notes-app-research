# Fact-check — handwriting recognition, sync, local AI, Nostr claims (lane 6)

**Date:** 2026-10-27 · **Rows:** 37 · **CONFIRMED:** 30 (4 with caveats noted) · **WRONG:** 1 · **UNSURE:** 6

Summary: The TrOCR IAM numbers (2.89 / 3.42 / 4.22 CER), the Tesseract 49.58% figure, the Supernote/MyScript repo claims, the sync-stack claims and most local-AI license claims all check out against primary sources, with small precision caveats (BeeKEM is a group key-agreement protocol, not a sync protocol; Piper is GPL-3.0 only in its current form; the Tesseract figure comes from okraPDF's own vendor benchmark). The one clear error is NIP-77: it is Negentropy Syncing, not "negative queries" (that is an NIP-50 extension). Six sub-claims are UNSURE — the nostr relay counts, "protocol-e2ee", whisper.cpp's "5x slower on Android", Anytype's iroh adoption, the nostr crate's monthly download figure (version itself confirmed), and the Evernote 57% sentiment stat — because no source could be reached (GitHub pages not fetchable in this session, web-search budget exhausted; crates.io/npm APIs were used for the Rust/npm package checks).
