# Fact-check: note-taking apps (Lane 1)

**Summary:** 14 rows checked against primary sources (official pricing pages, app-store listings, vendor blogs/docs, GitHub/Snapcraft/AUR listings) on 2026-10-27. **11 CONFIRMED, 3 WRONG (Goodnotes annual price; Flexcil "subscription"; MarginNote 4 "no subscription"), 1 UNSURE (CollaNote open-source — no public repo found, leaning not).** Amphi is two genuinely distinct products: Amphi Notes (amphi2024 org, GPL-3.0-or-later, self-hosted sync) and Amphi/useamphi.com (Amphi Labs LTD, closed-source iPad study canvas with AI and in-app purchases) — the two-product reading is correct.
