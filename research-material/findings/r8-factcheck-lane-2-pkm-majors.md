# Fact-check: note-taking apps research file (lane 2)

Checked 2026-10-27 against primary sources (official pricing/docs pages, vendor blogs, GitHub repos/registries). Third-party sources used only where noted and labeled.

**Summary:** 15 claims checked. 8 CONFIRMED, 5 WRONG (one of them only partly wrong), 2 UNSURE. Notable: Obsidian's community plugin library has roughly doubled in 2026 to ~9,500 (not ~5,000), and the research file's Obsidian Sync plan prices are swapped ($96/yr is Plus, not Standard). The Elastic Security Labs incident is real and April 2026, but the delivery method and targets in the file are wrong.

| # | Claim (short) | Verdict | Correction/evidence | URL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Obsidian: proprietary, free for personal use, NOT open source | CONFIRMED | Obsidian is proprietary and not open source; the app is free to use (in fact free for all purposes, personal and commercial, since Feb 2025, so "free for personal use" understates it slightly). License overview: "Obsidian is free for all purposes" and proprietary. | https://obsidian.md/license |
| 2 | Sync Standard ≈96 USD/yr; Plus is default-selected at checkout | WRONG | Prices are swapped: Standard is $4/mo billed annually = $48/yr ($5/mo monthly); Plus is $8/mo billed annually = $96/yr ($10/mo monthly). $96/yr belongs to Plus. UNSURE on the "default-selected at checkout" part: not verifiable without an account/checkout session; official pages show Plus labeled "Popular" but state no checkout default. Note: obsidian.md/pricing now lists a single "Sync $4/mo (billed annually)" plan while obsidian.md/sync still shows Standard and Plus side by side. | https://obsidian.md/sync |
| 3 | Community plugin count roughly 5,000 | WRONG | Current official registry shows ~9,521 plugins (and 940 themes) on the official community page; the official community-plugins.json in obsidianmd/obsidian-releases contained 9,522 entries on 2026-10-27. Third-party reporting (XDA, Oct 2026) says the library doubled to over 8,000+ within 2026. The file's figure is roughly a year out of date. | https://community.obsidian.md/ |
| 4 | JSON Canvas spun out by Obsidian team, MIT, ~March 2024 | CONFIRMED | jsoncanvas.org launched March 11, 2024; spec and site under MIT license (jsoncanvas/jsoncanvas GitHub repo, LICENSE = MIT); built/maintained by the Obsidian team (Steph Ango/kepano et al.). | https://github.com/jsoncanvas/jsoncanvas |
| 5 | April 2026: RAT distributed via fake/malicious Obsidian app downloads, targeting Ukraine-related government/military users (Elastic Security Labs) | WRONG | Month and publisher are right, but the details are not. Elastic Security Labs (April 2026) reported a campaign abusing Obsidian's community plugin ecosystem: attackers shared malicious vaults whose export/import auto-executed the Shell Commands plugin to drop PhantomPulse RAT; targets were individuals in the financial and cryptocurrency sectors recruited via LinkedIn/Telegram. Not fake app downloads, and not Ukraine government/military targets. (Labeled: no alternative Elastic report matching the Ukraine framing was found.) | https://www.elastic.co/security-labs/obsidian-rat-abuses-plugin-ecosystem |
| 6 | Tasks plugin had a 2026 security/RCE issue | CONFIRMED | Obsidian official security advisory ADR-002 (Tasks security vulnerability): reported 2026-04-20; RCE possible because Tasks queries could run JavaScript from vault data (CVSS 8.6); fixed in Tasks 8.0.0 released 2026-05-08, which disables JS execution in queries by default (re-enableable under "Advanced" settings). | https://obsidian.md/changelog/adr-002/ |
| 7 | Notion: offline exists but per-page; closed source | CONFIRMED | Notion's docs: pages can be marked "Available offline" one at a time (desktop/mobile); paid plans additionally auto-download recently visited and favorited pages, not the whole workspace; user requests for a full-workspace offline toggle remain open. Notion is closed source/proprietary (no public source code or license; product runs as a hosted service). | https://www.notion.com/help/offline-use-and-notion |
| 8 | "2 pages = 1.5 GB" user-measured disk usage | UNSURE | No public source found backing a "2 pages = 1.5 GB" figure (searched web, Notion community, Reddit). Related but different: user reports of the Notion desktop cache ballooning to multiple GB (e.g. a 12 GB "Partitions" cache folder blamed on offline-mode caching), which is cache size, not per-page storage. Third-party (Reddit) — the specific ratio remains unverified. | https://www.reddit.com/r/Notion/comments/1qbj4l8/whats_inside_this_12gb_folder_in_notion/ |
| 9 | Evernote free: 50 notes, 1 notebook; Bending Spoons; layoffs; price hikes | CONFIRMED (one fix) | Official Evernote help/pricing: free plan limited to 50 notes and 1 notebook — the file's "51-note cap" is off by one; it is 50. Owned by Bending Spoons (acquisition closed Jan 2023); layoffs under Bending Spoons (e.g. Feb 2023 and June 2024, with US staff cut to a skeleton team); multiple large price increases since 2023 (Personal roughly $70/yr → $129.99/yr, and a further 2025 restructure: Starter $99/yr, Advanced $249.99/yr). | https://help.evernote.com/hc/en-us/articles/209005897 |
| 10 | 57% of Evernote US App Store reviews negative | UNSURE | No primary or named-analytics source found for a "57% negative" figure. Conflicting third-party numbers exist (one site lists 33.3% negative; an analyst notes ~50% of reviews are 1–3 stars), while the current US App Store rating is high (about 4.4/5 over ~78k ratings), which argues against a majority-negative share. Percentages like this also churn with review volume. Labeled third-party and contradictory: treat 57% as unsupported. | https://apps.apple.com/us/app/evernote/id281796108 |
| 11 | OneNote closed source; sync complaints; Samsung Notes↔OneNote sync ends July 2026 | CONFIRMED | OneNote/Office are closed source (proprietary Microsoft product). Sync pain is a long-standing, widely documented complaint (not from a primary source, but not disputed). The Samsung partnership claim holds: Samsung's official support page says the Samsung Notes–OneNote sync feature (offered since 2020) will no longer be available after July 2026. | https://www.samsung.com/us/support/answer/ANC10002143/ |
| 12 | Tana personal pricing ~10–18 USD/mo; markdown export limits then improvement; TanaMigrator exists | WRONG (pricing) | Tana restructured pricing in 2026: tana.inc/pricing now lists Free / Pro at $20/mo early bird ($30 regular) / Business from $35/user/mo; the old Plus $10 and Pro $18 monthly tiers no longer exist. A separate product line, "Tana Outliner," lists Plus at $8/mo. Markdown-export part is correct: export was historically JSON-only (markdown-limited), and a first-class Export-as-Markdown shipped in May 2025 (team announcement). TanaMigrator is real: github.com/glibalien/tanamigrator (community tool by a Tana user, not official). | https://tana.inc/pricing |
| 13 | Reflect ≈10 USD/mo; "Reflect Open" announced 2026 | CONFIRMED | Reflect still bills existing users $10/mo (annual plan); new purchases run about $19/mo. "Reflect Open" was announced July 14, 2026: a local-first, open-source, markdown-based rebuild of Reflect (Mac app free during open beta, MIT-licensed code); it is open source, not merely an open protocol. | https://reflect.app/ |
| 14 | Anytype 0.47.4 improved export ~July 2025; "Any Source Available" license; source-available not OSI; E2EE | CONFIRMED | Release 0.47.4 shipped June 27, 2025 with import/export of properties and types for Markdown (announced to users ~July 17, 2025). LICENSE.md in anyproto/anytype-ts is titled "Any Source Available License 1.0": source-available but with a non-commercial-use restriction, so not OSI open source. Anytype markets end-to-end encryption for client-stored content (nuance: their docs say the business cloud on Anytype's servers is not zero-knowledge; the E2EE claim applies to personal vaults). | https://raw.githubusercontent.com/anyproto/anytype-ts/main/LICENSE.md |
| 15 | Logseq: AGPL-3.0; issue #3457 = outliner performance; April 2024 post on DB rewrite; file version in maintenance, DB in beta; hourly backups | PARTLY WRONG | AGPL-3.0 confirmed (logseq/LICENSE.md). April 2024: official announcement "Why the database version and how it's going?" posted April 29, 2024 (forum announcement, not a blog post) explaining the DB rewrite. Maintenance mode: official announcement of April 24, 2026 split Logseq into "Logseq OG" (file-based, new repo logseq/og, maintenance-only: security fixes and Electron/dependency upgrades) and the DB version; first public DB beta shipped July 13, 2026. WRONG on issue #3457: that issue is a 2020 markdown formatting bug ("- added at the start of blocks"), not the outliner performance issue; the often-cited performance issues live under other numbers (e.g. #8137, editing massive pages). Backups: official docs recommend automated/regular backups of the db.sqlite; the specific "hourly" cadence is community advice and unverified as official. | https://github.com/logseq/logseq/issues/3457 |
